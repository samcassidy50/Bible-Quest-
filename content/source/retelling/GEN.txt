GEN 1:8 God called the space “sky.” There was evening, and there was morning. That was the second day.

GEN 1:10 God called the dry land “earth,” and He called the gathered waters “seas.” God saw that it was good.
GEN 1:11 Then God said, “Let the earth grow grass, plants with seeds, and trees with fruit that have seeds inside, each of its own kind.” And it was so.
GEN 1:12 The earth grew grass and plants with seeds, each of its own kind, and trees with fruit that had seeds inside, each of its own kind. God saw that it was good.
GEN 1:13 There was evening, and there was morning. That was the third day.
GEN 1:14 Then God said, “Let there be lights in the sky to set the day apart from the night. Let them be signs to mark seasons, days, and years.
GEN 1:15 Let them be lights in the sky to shine on the earth.” And it was so.
GEN 1:16 God made two great lights: the bigger light to rule the day, and the smaller light to rule the night. He made the stars too.
GEN 1:17 God set them in the sky to give light to the earth,
GEN 1:18 to rule over the day and the night, and to set the light apart from the darkness. God saw that it was good.
GEN 1:19 There was evening, and there was morning. That was the fourth day.
GEN 1:20 Then God said, “Let the waters be full of living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth across the sky.”
GEN 1:21 So God made the great sea creatures and every living thing that swims in the waters, each of its own kind, and every bird with wings, each of its own kind. God saw that it was good.
GEN 1:22 God blessed them and said, “Have many young and fill the waters of the seas, and let the birds grow in number on the earth.”
GEN 1:23 There was evening, and there was morning. That was the fifth day.
GEN 1:24 Then God said, “Let the earth bring out living creatures, each of its own kind: farm animals, creatures that crawl, and wild animals.” And it was so.
GEN 1:25 God made the wild animals, the farm animals, and every creature that crawls on the ground, each of its own kind. God saw that it was good.
GEN 1:26 Then God said, “Let Us make people in Our image, to be like Us. They will rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky, over the farm animals, and over all the earth and every creature that crawls on it.”
GEN 1:27 So God made people in His own image. In the image of God He made them. He made them male and female.
GEN 1:28 God blessed them and said to them, “Have many children and fill the earth, and take charge of it. Rule over the fish in the sea, the birds in the sky, and every living thing that moves on the earth.”
GEN 1:29 God said, “Look, I have given you every plant with seeds on the whole earth, and every tree that has fruit with seeds. They will be your food.
GEN 1:30 And I have given every green plant as food to every animal on the earth, every bird in the sky, and every creature that crawls on the ground, everything that has life.” And it was so.
GEN 1:31 God looked at everything He had made, and it was very good. There was evening, and there was morning. That was the sixth day.
