GEN 1:1 In the beginning, God made the heavens and the earth.
GEN 1:2 The earth had no shape, and it was empty. Darkness covered the deep waters. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the waters.
GEN 1:3 God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light.
GEN 1:4 God saw that the light was good. He set the light apart from the darkness.
GEN 1:5 God called the light “day,” and He called the darkness “night.” There was evening, and there was morning. That was the first day.
GEN 1:26 Then God said, “Let Us make people in Our image, to be like Us. They will rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky, over the farm animals, and over all the earth and every creature that crawls on it.”
GEN 1:27 So God made people in His own image. In the image of God He made them. He made them male and female.
GEN 2:15 Then the Lord God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it.
GEN 2:16 The Lord God told him, “You may eat from any tree in the garden,
GEN 2:17 but you must not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. If you eat from it, you will surely die.”
GEN 3:1 The serpent was more tricky than any other wild animal the Lord God had made. He said to the woman, “Did God really say, ‘You must not eat from any tree in the garden’?”
GEN 3:2 The woman answered the serpent, “We may eat fruit from the trees in the garden,
GEN 3:3 but God said, ‘You must not eat fruit from the tree in the middle of the garden. Do not even touch it, or you will die.’”
GEN 3:4 “You will not surely die,” the serpent told the woman.
GEN 3:5 “God knows that when you eat from it, your eyes will be opened. You will be like God, knowing good and evil.”
GEN 3:6 The woman saw that the fruit of the tree was good for food and nice to look at. She wanted it, hoping it would make her wise. So she took some fruit and ate it. She also gave some to her husband, who was with her, and he ate it.
GEN 3:17 Then God said to Adam, “You listened to your wife and ate from the tree I told you not to eat from. So the ground is cursed because of you. You will work hard to get food from it all the days of your life.
GEN 3:18 It will grow thorns and thistles for you, and you will eat the plants of the field.
GEN 3:19 You will sweat and work hard for your bread until you go back to the ground, because you were made from it. You are dust, and you will go back to dust.”
GEN 4:6 Then the Lord said to Cain, “Why are you angry? Why do you look so upset?
GEN 4:7 If you do what is right, won’t you be accepted? But if you don’t do what is right, sin is crouching at your door. It wants to have you, but you must rule over it.”
GEN 6:13 Then God said to Noah, “I am going to put an end to all living things, because people have filled the earth with fighting and hurting. I will destroy them along with the earth.
GEN 6:14 Build yourself an ark out of gopher wood. Make rooms inside it, and cover it with tar inside and out.
GEN 7:11 When Noah was six hundred years old, on the seventeenth day of the second month, all the springs deep under the earth burst open, and the floodgates of the sky were opened.
GEN 7:12 Rain fell on the earth for forty days and forty nights.
GEN 9:12 God said, “This is the sign of the promise I am making with you and with every living creature with you, a promise for all time to come:
GEN 9:13 I have put My rainbow in the clouds. It will be the sign of the promise between Me and the earth.
GEN 11:4 Then the people said, “Come, let’s build a city for ourselves, with a tower that reaches up to the heavens. Then we will become famous, and we will not be scattered all over the earth.”
GEN 11:5 But the Lord came down to see the city and the tower the people were building.
GEN 11:6 The Lord said, “They are one people, and they all speak the same language. If they have started doing this, nothing they plan will be too hard for them.
GEN 11:7 Come, let Us go down and mix up their language, so they will not understand each other.”
