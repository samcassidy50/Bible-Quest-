# Genesis, every passage: encounter plan (DRAFT)

A plan to read **all of Genesis, in order** (1,533 verses) across **169 encounters**, about 9 verses
each (roughly a minute of narration at ~8 s a verse). Each encounter stays inside one chapter
(`parsePassage` only takes single-chapter ranges) and breaks where the story breaks.

**The "Kid-friendly text" column is a short retelling of what the player reads in that encounter**,
so you can judge the split and the tone. The in-game text is still retold **verse by verse** into
`content/source/retelling/GEN.txt` from a public-domain translation (BSB/WEB) open beside the
writer, as the content rules require. Everything here is an AI draft for a human to review.

**Built so far (2026-11-26):** encounters 1–11 (the Beginnings arc), then a choice of three paths
on the map: 19–27 (Noah), 31 (Babel) and 52–54 (a short side path: Sodom's destruction). Each encounter's id is its number here (`gen-019`). The
others are left out for now, as picked in the Genesis Passage Picker.

**Flags** (nothing is skipped unless you say so):

- 📜 **Family list.** Mostly names and ages. Could be read as-is, shortened, or skipped.
- ⚠️ **Grown-up moment.** Told gently below; worth a careful human read.
- ⚠️⚠️ **Hard for children.** Even told gently, I'd suggest skipping (or summing up in one line).
